## Label Definition

Positive label: `watch_ratio >= positive_watch_ratio_threshold` after adapter normalization. Default threshold is `1.0`.

## Split Strategy

Interactions are split per user by timestamp into train/val/test. Evaluation filters past positive items from `train,val`.

## Candidate Recall Before Ranker

The ranker is trained on candidate-level samples generated by real retrievers and RRF fusion. Missing split positives are injected only as audited supervision rows; they are not exposed as a `label_positive` retrieval-source embedding.

Important rerun note: an earlier real-data run is invalid because KuaiRec numeric Unix-second timestamps were parsed with pandas' default nanosecond unit, collapsing processed timestamps to `1` and corrupting the temporal split. After the timestamp fix, test candidate positive recall is `0.098848` instead of the invalid earlier near-zero value. The remaining bottleneck is real recall coverage, especially cold/tail items.

## Known Limitations

- Offline Recall/NDCG/MRR/MAP do not prove online CTR, watch-time, or retention lift.
- The local neural retrievers/rankers are compact interview implementations, not distributed production training jobs.
- Full KuaiRec results require real raw data under `data/raw/kuairec`; sample fallback is only a smoke-test path and is marked in `meta.json`.
- This run uses official real data from `data/raw/kuairec_official/extracted/KuaiRec 2.0/data`; `data/raw/kuairec` may still contain simulated development data and should not be used for official claims.
- Positive injection makes ranker supervision possible when retrieval misses labels, but a high injection rate means the recall stage still needs improvement.
- The ranker samples currently load through pandas row access, which is slow for repeated ablations; tensor-cache or Arrow-batch loading is a priority engineering improvement.
- Cold-start and tail analysis here are offline buckets, not production traffic cohorts.
- The LLM guardrail is a deterministic safety smoke test, not a complete red-team evaluation.
